# Pillars and anti-goals

Signed by Kristian on 2026-11-16. Changes only at a milestone, through a numbered decision file.

Amended 2026-11-19, outside a milestone: the wording of pillar 1 went from right-click movement and attack-move to W A S D and cursor aim, through decisions 0002 and 0003, after Kristian had played and confirmed W A S D. The producer wrote the new wording and told him; he has not signed it.

A new mechanic must support at least one pillar, or it is not built.

| # | Pillar | Like | Not like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Precise, committed control.** W A S D to move, the basic shot and the skills aimed at the cursor, dash is a scarce resource. | Lost Ark: dash on a long cooldown, every skill has a phase you are locked into. Reference class for feel: Gunslinger. | Hades: a dash you can use almost freely. |
| 2 | **The class is an instrument.** Eight skills, an identity gauge and tripods that play together. | A Lost Ark class with a rotation and its own resource. | A weapon with three attacks. |
| 3 | **The build grows inside the run.** Tripods and skills are rewards along the way. Meta mostly opens new choices, plus some raw stats in noticeable, capped steps. | Shape of Dreams, Arcana in Hades II. | Permanent upgrades of a few percent that cannot be felt. |
| 4 | **Readable combat.** Telegraphs, stagger and counter make you lose to your own mistakes. | Lost Ark bosses with colour-coded telegraphs. | A screen full of effects you cannot read. |

## Anti-goals

- No multiplayer.
- No gear or loot with stats.
- No open world or town.
- No story or dialogue before the demo.
- No second class before the demo.

## Fixed facts

- Isometric roguelike, full 3D, steady meta progression.
- Character control and class depth like Lost Ark.
- First class: a shooter with skill shots.
- Death ends the run.
- Goal: a release on Steam. First public milestone: a free demo.
- Kristian has 3 to 7 hours a week. His play sessions set the pace.
