# Pillars and anti-goals

First signed by Kristian on 2026-11-16. Changes through a numbered decision file.

**Rewritten 2026-11-19 through decision 0006.** Kristian approved the four pillar statements ("pilarene ser bra ut"). The "Like" and "Not like" cells are carried over from the first version or written by the producer, and he has not read them. The numbers changed: documents from before this date use the old ones, in which pillar 2 was the instrument and pillar 3 the build that grows inside the run.

A new mechanic must support at least one pillar, or it is not built.

| # | Pillar | Like | Not like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fast, precise control.** W A S D to move, everything aimed at the cursor. Light skills fire on the move; heavy skills commit her, and the size of the hit pays for it. | Lost Ark's Gunslinger for movement and skill timing. | A game where every skill roots you in place. |
| 2 | **High tempo with skill expression.** Many presses a minute, and timing and combos set a good player apart from a middling one. | The fast playstyles of Lost Ark: skills pressed again inside a window, releases that have to be timed. | A rotation of a few long cooldowns with waiting in between. |
| 3 | **The class is an instrument built during the run.** Four skills at the start, eight at the end, an identity gauge, and upgrades that change what a skill is for. Meta mostly opens new choices, plus some raw stats in noticeable, capped steps. | A Lost Ark class with a rotation and its own resource. Shape of Dreams, Arcana in Hades II. | A weapon with three attacks. Permanent upgrades of a few percent that cannot be felt. |
| 4 | **Readable combat.** Telegraphs, stagger and counter make you lose to your own mistakes. | Lost Ark bosses with colour-coded telegraphs. | A screen full of effects you cannot read. |

## Anti-goals

- No multiplayer.
- No gear or loot with stats.
- No open world or town.
- No story or dialogue before the demo.
- No second class before the demo.

## Fixed facts

- Isometric roguelike, full 3D, steady meta progression.
- Character control and class depth like Lost Ark.
- First class: a shooter with skill shots.
- Death ends the run.
- Goal: a release on Steam. First public milestone: a free demo.
- Kristian has 3 to 7 hours a week. His play sessions set the pace.
