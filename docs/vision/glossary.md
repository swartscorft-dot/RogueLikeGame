# Words we use

Kristian asked for agreed terms on 2026-11-19, after playing a generated level with the new kit: "jeg tror også vi må bli enige om ordbruk, terminologier her." This list is the producer's proposal from the same evening. He has not confirmed it; until he does, treat every term as a suggestion. The Norwegian word is the one to use when talking with him.

| Term | Norwegian | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Run** | løp | One attempt, from the start of act 1 to death or to the last boss. Death ends the run. |
| **Act** | akt | A chapter of a run with one biome and one boss at its end. Act 1 is the Crypt. |
| **Floor** | etasje | One generated layout: a chain of rooms played from its entrance to its exit. What was built on 2026-11-19 and called a "level" is one floor. His comparison: a floor in Slay the Spire. |
| **Room** | rom | One walled space on a floor. |
| **Combat room** | kamprom | A room with enemies. |
| **Treasure room** | skattkammer | A small side room with a chest. Called "reward room" in the code and notes until now. |
| **Chest** | skattekiste | Gives a small upgrade to a stat. His words: "skattekistene kan være minor stat upgrades". |
| **Elite room** | eliterom | The one hard room in the middle of a floor. Clearing it gives a skill upgrade, one of three. Decided by him on 2026-11-20. |
| **Elite** | elite | The tougher, larger enemy that an elite room holds besides its ordinary enemies. |
| **Exit room** | utgangsrom | The last room of a floor, with its hardest ordinary fight. On an act's last floor it holds the act boss. |
| **Floor reward** | etasjebelønning | The strong reward for finishing a floor: a new skill, or something of that weight. His words: "en relativt sterk belønning (som nytt spell osv)". |
| **Act boss** | aktboss | The boss that ends an act. |
| **Seed** | frø | The eight characters a floor is generated from. The same seed gives the same floor. |
| **Kit** | sett | Everything the character can do: the basic shot, the skills, the dash, the gauge. |
| **Skill** | ferdighet | One of up to eight things on the skill keys. He also says "spell". A run starts with four and takes four more from the pool. |
| **Pool** | pott | The eight skills that the four free keys are filled from during a run, one of three offered after each floor. |
| **Mark** | merke | What pistol skills put on an enemy and shotgun skills spend. He says "stacks". |
| **Focus** | fokus | The gauge on Z. |
| **Upgrade** | oppgradering | A change to one skill chosen during a run, from an elite room. Lost Ark's word, used in the first documents, is "tripod". The small steps from chests are "chest upgrades" (kisteoppgraderinger). |
| **Caster** | besverger | The fifth kind of enemy: it bursts circles on the ground where she stands. The others: brute (skjelettkriger), swarmer (ghoul), shooter (bueskytter), guard (vakt). |
| **Turn to attack** | tur til å angripe | The rule that only a few enemies of a floor may wind up at the same moment. |
| **Rack** | hylle | In the between-runs trial: the pieces she owns, of which she equips as many as her budget of points allows. |
| **Piece** | brikke | One thing on the rack: a rule that changes for the next run, or a capped stat step. |
| **Feat** | bragd | Something done once that raises the rack's budget by a point. |
| **Pack** | flokk | Several weak enemies that wake and fight together. |
| **Cover** | dekning | A wall or a tall prop that stops shots. |
| **Practice room** | treningsrom | The hand-built room with the dummies. Not part of a run. |

## Words to stop using

- "Level" for a floor: it also means character level in most games.
- "Reward room" for the treasure room: the floor reward is the big reward, and it is not in that room.
- "Boss" for the last enemy of a floor: a floor ends with its hardest room; the boss is the act's.
