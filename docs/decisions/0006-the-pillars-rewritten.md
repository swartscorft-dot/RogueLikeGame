# 0006 The pillars rewritten

Status: **the four pillar statements were approved by Kristian on 2026-11-19: "pilarene ser bra ut" (the pillars look good).** He read them as a draft in Norwegian in the session; the English wording in `docs/vision/pillars.md` is the producer's translation of that draft. The "Like" and "Not like" cells there are carried over or written by the producer, and he has not read them.

## Why

The pillars were signed on 2026-11-16, before anything had been played. Three days of play and one round of research moved the game away from them:

- Pillar 1 said every skill has a phase you are locked into and the dash is a scarce resource. On 2026-11-19 he ruled that light skills may fire on the move and only the heavy ones lock her, and said of the conflict: "drit i pilar 1. de må skrives om uansett" (never mind pillar 1; they have to be rewritten anyway).
- Nothing in the pillars said the game is fast. He said twice the same day that a high tempo is what he likes best ("veldig glad i høy apm").
- Pillar 2 said eight skills, pillar 3 said skills are rewards along the way. He decided that a run starts with four skills and ends with eight, which makes the two one thing.
- After reading the kit proposal he said: "vi må jobbe med pilarene igjen ser jeg" (I see we have to work on the pillars again).

## The change

| Old | New |
|---|---|
| 1. Precise, committed control. W A S D to move, the basic shot and the skills aimed at the cursor, dash is a scarce resource. | 1. **Fast, precise control.** W A S D, everything aimed with the mouse. Light skills fire on the move; heavy skills commit you, and the hit pays for it. |
| (none) | 2. **High tempo with skill expression.** Many presses a minute, and timing and combos set a good player apart from a middling one. |
| 2. The class is an instrument. Eight skills, an identity gauge and tripods that play together. | 3. **The class is an instrument built during the run.** Four skills at the start, eight at the end, a gauge, and upgrades that change what a skill is for. |
| 3. The build grows inside the run. Tripods and skills are rewards along the way. Meta mostly opens new choices, plus some raw stats in noticeable, capped steps. | Merged into the new 3. The sentence about meta progression is carried over unchanged. |
| 4. Readable combat. Telegraphs, stagger and counter make you lose to your own mistakes. | 4. Unchanged. |

## What is left open by this

- **The dash.** The old pillar 1 called it a scarce resource; the new one does not mention it. Whether it stays scarce is an open question in decision 0005, and the game is unchanged: one dash on a long cooldown.
- **The numbers of the pillars changed.** Older documents (the slices, decisions 0001 to 0005, the playtest notes) use the old numbers: there, pillar 2 is the instrument and pillar 3 the build that grows. They are not rewritten.
- The anti-goals and the fixed facts are unchanged.
