# 0007 Generated levels come off the cut list

Status: **decided by Kristian on 2026-11-19: "jeg synes det skal bygges før demoen" (I think it should be built before the demo).**

## What changed

`docs/vision/cut-list.md` listed "Procedurally generated rooms" among the things deliberately not built before the demo. That line is removed.

## How it came about

- The same day he asked for a start on levels generated from a seed, and another session of his had begun making modular props for them. The producer pointed out that this went against the cut list and asked whether the item should come off it. His answer is the line above.
- The first version was built that day in work order 006 and merged (55e0a32): a generator and a validator as rules, a seed shown as eight characters, rooms side by side on a 4 m grid, and a grey-box level that can be walked with enemies in it. He ran through it the same evening: "nivåene virker greit, vanskelig å dømme i dette stadiet med de samme fiendene. Men jeg føler det er inne på noe" (the levels seem fine; hard to judge at this stage with the same enemies; but I feel it is onto something). He gave no verdict on the grid size, the wall height or the room sizes.

## What it does not change

- The other items on the cut list stand, among them "More than one environment" and "Production art before the art test". The first biome, the Crypt, is the one environment. The Meshy props made the same day are a first batch in one held style, not finished production art.
- Pillar 3 is unchanged: the build grows inside the run. Generated levels are where the run happens, and its four reward rooms are where skills five to eight are meant to be handed out.

## The cost, stated once

His play time is 3 to 7 hours a week and sets the pace. Generated levels add a second large thing to judge by playing, beside the kit. The grid size, the wall height and the room sizes are all guesses until he has run through a level.
