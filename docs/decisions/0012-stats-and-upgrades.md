# 0012 Stats and upgrades: attack speed, flat damage, crit, luck and tiers

Status: **the direction is Kristian's, said on 2026-11-20 after his third session. The list of stats and the numbers are the producer's proposal, which he asked for ("hvilke andre stats og lignende tenker du bør være med"); a first version is built from it behind a switch. He has not played it.**

## What he said

"Det må være større variasjon i upgrade. Jeg ser for meg flere stats. Vi har ikke attack speed. Så jeg tenker vi bør inkorporere det på et vis. Starte litt tregere enn det vi gjør fra start. Men stats kan øke denne, altså hvor fort man kan trykke. Jeg vil heller at vi får flat dmg fra chest. 5% kan spares til litt sterkere ting senere. Hvilke andre stats og lignende tenker du bør være med, armor? Vi har jo MS, crit/crit dmg kan være nice. Luck? Vi har ikke noe tiers på upgrades og spells. Men det kunne være noe vi så litt mer på."

And for later: "En idé til metaprogresjon kan være attachments til våpen. Laser-sikte = bedre range, osv. Noe å ta med til senere."

## What follows

1. **Attack speed is a stat.** It is how fast she can shoot: the time between basic shots. She starts a little slower than she does today, and upgrades buy it back and beyond.
2. **A chest gives flat steps**, not percentages: a number added, not a share. Percentages are kept for the rarer, stronger things.
3. **More stats, so that upgrades differ.**
4. **Tiers** on upgrades, so that the same kind of thing can come small or large; and **luck** to lean on them.

## The producer's proposal

### The stats

| Stat | What it does | Where she starts | Why it is in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Damage** | Added flat to every basic shot; a skill adds it times its own weight (a heavy skill counts it more than a quick one). | 0 added | His words: flat damage from chests. |
| **Attack speed** | The basic shot's rate. Not the skills' cooldowns, and not the timing windows (a clean press keeps its rhythm whatever her speed). | about 15 % slower than today | His words. Kept off the windows so that timing stays a skill of the hands. |
| **Crit chance** | The chance that a hit is a crit. **A clean press and a perfect shot are always a crit:** playing well is the reliable way to crit, luck is the other. | 5 % | It ties a gunslinger's stat to pillar 2 (timing). |
| **Crit damage** | How much more a crit deals. | +50 % | The partner of crit chance: two stats that are only good together make builds. |
| **Move speed** | As today. | as today | He named it. |
| **Max health** | As today (+10 a step). | as today | The plain defensive stat. |
| **Cooldown** | Skill and dash cooldowns shorter, as today's steps. | as today | The skill-build stat. |
| **Range** | Reach of shots, as today's +1 m. | as today | Also the stat his "laser sight" attachment would give between runs. |
| **Stagger** | As today. | as today | The stat against guards and elites. |
| **Luck** | Leans every offer toward the higher tiers. | 0 | It only means something with tiers, and tiers want it. |

**Not armour, for now.** Armour makes each hit hurt less, which pays her for being hit; the game is about not being hit, and its small fast hits (a swarmer's bite of 1 or 2) would vanish behind any flat armour. If a second defensive stat is wanted later, the better fit is one that rewards the dodge: for example a short guard after a dash or a duck that takes the edge off the next hit.

### Tiers

Three tiers, shown by colour and name on the card: **common** (a small step), **rare** (about twice it), **epic** (about three times it, or a percentage, or a rule that changes how something works). A chest mostly offers commons; the elite room's reward is rare or better; a floor's reward can be epic. Luck moves all of it up. The percentage steps that exist today (damage +5 % and the like) become the epic end.

Skills get tiers later and in their own way (the elite rooms' skill upgrades are already a second layer on a skill); nothing of that is built here.

### For the between-runs design (decision 0009)

Weapon attachments as the things one owns between runs: a laser sight for range, a longer barrel, a lighter trigger for attack speed, and so on. Each is a small permanent lean toward a stat, which is what 0009's "pieces on a rack" were meant to be; attachments give them a face. Noted, not built.
