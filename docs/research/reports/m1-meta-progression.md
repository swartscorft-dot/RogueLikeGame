# M1: What lasts between runs in action roguelikes

Research date: 20 November 2026. Each claim links to a page I opened. Where I only saw a search-result summary and could not open the page, the claim is marked **not verified**. Many details come from fan wikis and guides; their game-version notes are given where the page states one. Fandom wikis, SteamDB, Twinfinite and the full GamesRadar Hades II interview were blocked or truncated, which caused some gaps; these are listed in section 6.

---

## 0. Short version

- **Two families.** In one, lasting progress opens choices: unlocks into the pool, loadout pieces, alternative versions, difficulty settings (Risk of Rain 2, Slay the Spire, Wizard of Legend 2, Shape of Dreams after its rework). In the other it is mainly numbers (Rogue Legacy 2's manor, Vampire Survivors' PowerUps, Dead Cells' Blacksmith). Hades and Hades II mix both, but put the numbers behind a **choice limit**: Mirror pairs where only one side is active, and the Grasp budget on Arcana.
- **The clearest model for "opens choices, plus capped stats":** Hades II's Arcana. It has 25 cards, a Grasp budget that rises from 10 to 30, and a full set that costs 56 Grasp, so "you can never run them all at once". The second clearest is Shape of Dreams' 2025 rework. Its constellation stars stopped being "permanent upgrades" and became items you equip in typed slots, some plain stats and some that "change the way you play a character".
- **The closest structural match to this project** (a skill kit chosen before the run) is Wizard of Legend 2. Unlocking a skill into the pool and choosing the starting loadout are separate stations there, and difficulty is a set of runes that trades positive for negative effects against +0–50% meta currency.
- **The failure the sources describe most often is pacing, not structure.** Rogue Legacy 2 needed a "massive economy update ... purely aimed at reducing the grind". Shape of Dreams patched "excessive growth stress" on its launch day and cut mastery requirements by 30% three weeks later.
- **On raw power the developers really do disagree.** Supergiant made damage resistance grow with every death ("What if we just make you a little bit tougher?"), and lists permanent progression as an accessibility tool. Hopoo designs so you "can absolutely 'win' ... on a fresh install", and made later unlocks "not strictly power upgrades". A designer argument from 2015 says unlocks "weaken the core loop of die, analyze, regroup".
- **Pool dilution:** the answers found are an opt-in mode that limits the pool (Dead Cells), bought Reroll/Skip/Banish/Seal tools (Vampire Survivors), designing new items so they don't make old ones obsolete (Hopoo), and keeping the pool size per character modest (Slay the Spire, about 75 cards).

## 1. Game by game

### 1.1 Hades (Supergiant, v1.0 September 2020)

**Earned in a run:** Darkness and Chthonic Keys drop during runs ([NoobFeed, Dec 2018](https://noobfeed.com/articles/hades-early-access-preview)). Titan Blood comes from bosses ([Fextralife: Infernal Arms](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Infernal+Arms)). Gold (Obols) is spent only inside the run ([NoobFeed](https://noobfeed.com/articles/hades-early-access-preview)). Whether a lost run keeps its Darkness: **not verified** in a page I opened.

**Spent on, by category:**
- *Raw power and choices before a run:* The **Mirror of Night** has 12 talent pairs, and "You cannot use both a Talent and its alternate version" ([Fextralife: Mirror](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Mirror+of+the+Night), last edited June 2026, version not stated). Roughly half are plain numbers: Shadow Presence +10% backstab damage per rank, Thick Skin +5 life per rank × 10. Some are mechanics: Death Defiance revives, an extra dash, Fated Authority/Persuasion rerolls. Some are long chains of tiny steps: **Olympian Favor is +1% rare boon chance per rank, 40 ranks, 2,000 Darkness.** That is exactly the "few percent that cannot be felt" the project's pillars forbid. Maxing all 24 talents costs about 35,375 Darkness, and only one side of each pair can be active. Talent pairs are unlocked with Chthonic Keys in four batches (5/10/20/30) (same page). The early-access build already let you "reassign Darkness between traits" after a few deaths ([NoobFeed](https://noobfeed.com/articles/hades-early-access-preview)).
- *Choices before a run:* Six weapons unlocked with keys (3–8 each). "Every Infernal Arm offers four interchangeable forms known as Aspects", unlocked and levelled to 5 with Titan Blood, and they can "reshape the entire moveset" ([Fextralife: Infernal Arms](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Infernal+Arms)). **Keepsakes** come from gifting Nectar: 25 standard plus 6 companions, one equipped at a time plus one companion. They level by clearing rooms (rank 2 after 25 rooms, rank 3 after 50 more). A later house upgrade lets you swap between regions. Some steer the pool; Owl Pendant makes "the next Boon you find ... from Athena" ([Fextralife: Keepsakes](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Keepsakes)).
- *Difficulty the player turns up:* The **Pact of Punishment**, a "late-game system" ([Supergiant FAQ](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-faq/)). A fan analysis says it opens after beating the game. It lists conditions that add Heat, for example Hard Labor (+20% enemy damage per rank, max 5), and calls Heat 32+ "brutally hard" ([saschb2b analysis](https://saschb2b.github.io/game-mechanics/games/hades/meta-progression), third party). Raising Heat "unlocks new completion rewards, including Titan Blood" ([Fextralife: Infernal Arms](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Infernal+Arms)).
- *Difficulty the player turns down:* **God Mode**: "always available to make you more resilient to damage", and a little stronger with each death ([Supergiant FAQ](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-faq/)).

**Limit forcing a choice:** Mirror pairs (one of two), one keepsake per region, one weapon and aspect per run.

**How long it lasts:** No source gave hours. Cost data: about 35k Darkness to max the Mirror; 65 keys for the talent pairs.

**Developer statements:**
- Kasavin on God Mode: "What if we just make you a little bit tougher?" The team made the player tougher instead of making enemies weaker, so patterns are still learned. It starts with an "immediately palpable" 20% damage resistance. And: "If you could just blow through it, what's interesting about the game goes away." Also: "It's not a one size fits all solution." ([Inverse, Aug 2021](https://inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview))
- "It was an explicit goal of our early development, to take the pain out of dying and having to restart"; "You have to be compelled to explore further and feel the time you spent wasn't a waste of your time." ([Game Developer, Feb 2020, quoting RPS](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/how-supergiant-weaves-narrative-rewards-into-i-hades-i-cycle-of-perpetual-death))
- The FAQ lists "difficulty modifiers and permanent progression systems" as ways to open "the thrills of rogue-like games to more players" ([Supergiant FAQ](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-faq/)).

### 1.2 Hades II (Supergiant, early access May 2024, v1.0 September 25, 2025)

**Earned in a run:** Ashes, Psyche, Moon Dust, Bones, gathered reagents and others. Psyche sources include room rewards, the Fated List and a merchant ([Kotaku, May 2024](https://kotaku.com/hades-2-grasp-arcana-cards-how-to-increase-1851465948)). Lost run vs won run: **not verified**. The headline of a GamesRadar interview quotes the director: "If you don't finish the run, it doesn't mean that you failed" ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/hades/hades-2-director-challenges-roguelike-convention-if-you-dont-finish-the-run-it-doesnt-mean-that-you-failed/)). Only the headline was readable, so the body and date are **not verified**.

**Spent on:**
- *Raw power and changed rules, under a budget:* **Arcana cards.** There are 25 as of v1.0, unlocked with Ashes. Nine are visible at first, and each unlock reveals its neighbours on the grid. Each costs 0–5 Grasp. Grasp starts at 10 and is raised with Psyche (+2 per step, rising cost; [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/hades-2-grasp-arcana-cards-how-to-increase-1851465948): 392 Psyche from 10 to 20) to a maximum of 30. The full set costs 56, so "you can never run them all at once". Zero-cost cards must first be "awakened" by a condition. Card rarity is raised with Moon Dust through an incantation ([RogueRanker, 2026, v1.0](https://rogueranker.com/hades-2-arcana/)). Example effects: extra Death Defiance; more damage while you have none; more duo-boon chance; activate random inactive Arcana after a guardian kill (same page).
- *New options in the pool and new features:* **Incantations**, crafted at the Cauldron from gathered reagents, unlock "additions to your home base as well as changes to future runs". Examples: a gathering tool, a merchant, boon elements and their infusion bonuses ([Kotaku, May 7, 2024](https://kotaku.com/hades-2-incantations-unlock-first-tips-1851461926)). Wikipedia: they "enable new features to appear in future runs" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hades_II)).
- *Choices before a run:* weapons and their Aspects. A third-party page lists mechanical examples, such as the Witch's Staff special turning into a channelled beam ([saschb2b](https://saschb2b.github.io/game-mechanics/games/hades/meta-progression)). Aspect costs: **not verified**.
- *Difficulty the player turns up:* the Oath of the Unseen (vows that add Fear), said to unlock after defeating Chronos, with 16 vows whose rewards feed aspect upgrades. These are **not verified**: search summaries only, pages not opened.

**Limit forcing a choice:** Grasp. This is the cleanest example in the set.

**How long it lasts:** no source on hours. The structure makes it last: going from 10 to 30 Grasp costs a steeply rising amount of Psyche, and 56 > 30 means the choice never goes away.

**Developer statements:** no Supergiant statement on why Arcana replaced the Mirror was found.

### 1.3 Shape of Dreams (Lizard Smoothie / NEOWIZ, PC release September 2025, v1.4 September 2026)

**Earned in a run:** **Stardust**, plus **Traveler Mastery** points for the character played. Defeating Heroic Bosses gives bonus Stardust that grows with difficulty ("0/1/3/5 ⇒ 0/2/4/7", patch 1.0.5, Sep 17, 2025). Mastery milestones give "250 Stardust at levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30" (patch 1.0.7, Oct 2, 2025). Since patch 1.0.7, "You now receive all mastery rewards even if you exit the game, are disconnected, or experience a crash." All quotes come from the official Steam announcements, read through [Steam's news API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=2444750&count=25&maxlength=4000&feeds=steam_community_announcements&enddate=1762000000). No post states whether a failed run earns less than a cleared one: **not verified**.

**Spent on:**
- *Raw power and changed play, under a slot limit:* **Stars**, placed in a per-character constellation. "Stars can now be purchased with Star Dust" (launch post, Sep 11, 2025). Travel Log #2 (May 10, 2025) set the rule that stars "will no longer function as permanent upgrades, but instead will become a flexible system" that you equip in slots. Some stars are basic stat boosts, others "change the way you play a character". There are four slot types; a mismatched star works "at 50% efficiency", and Flexibility slots are wild cards. Builds can be saved and loaded ([Travel Log #2](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1799088287886291)). A reviewer: each character has "a pretty deep system of upgrades between runs", with general and character-specific stars ([Cubed3, Sep 2026](https://www.cubed3.com/games/reviews/pc/shape-of-dreams)).
- *New options:* "Leveling [Mastery] up unlocks more stars and grants special rewards." "New stars and additional slot counts will also be unlocked according to your level" (Travel Log #2 and launch post). Of nine Travelers, two are available at the start ([Cubed3](https://www.cubed3.com/games/reviews/pc/shape-of-dreams)). The v1.4 "Way of Stars" lets you "replace your Traveler's Identity Memory" and opens a new ending route (v1.4 post, Sep 17, 2026, [news API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=2444750&count=40&maxlength=2500&feeds=steam_community_announcements)).
- *No effect on play:* costumes and banners bought with Stardust, emblems and emotes.
- *Difficulty the player turns up:* difficulty tiers up to Nightmare. "Limbo consists of six difficulty tiers, each with its own special penalties", added in v1.1, December 2025. Rewards there are higher Stardust bonuses and a cosmetic emblem. "Only 0.077% of Travelers managed to conquer the highest level of Limbo" (Sep 2026) (same news API).

**Limit forcing a choice:** star slots per character, typed slots, and the 50% penalty for a wrong type.

**How long it lasts:** a run takes 90–120 minutes and "relatively few" upgrades come from one run, so progress "can feel like a grind" ([Cubed3](https://www.cubed3.com/games/reviews/pc/shape-of-dreams)).

**Developer statements:** the stars rework answered feedback "about the inconvenience of turning constellations on and off repeatedly" and aims to "enhance the fun of character customization" ([Travel Log #2](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1799088287886291)). On launch day (Sep 11, 2025): "We are easing the excessive growth stress related to Stardust and Traveler Mastery", and the Daydream system would "resolve resource shortages needed for growth". Three weeks later: "Required mastery points for levels 0–30 reduced by 30%" ([news API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=2444750&count=25&maxlength=4000&feeds=steam_community_announcements&enddate=1762000000)).

### 1.4 Dead Cells (Motion Twin / Evil Empire)

**Earned in a run:** cells and blueprints. Unspent cells are "lost upon death" ([wiki: Cells](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Cells)). "A blueprint is lost for the run when the player dies before delivering it to the Collector." Since v2.3, blueprints are handed in automatically at biome transitions ([wiki: Blueprints](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Blueprints)). So a lost run keeps only what was banked at checkpoints between biomes.

**Spent on:**
- *New options in the pool:* blueprints paid for in cells become items that "can appear throughout the game". There are 256 enemy-dropped blueprints plus 42 from secret areas ([wiki: Blueprints](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Blueprints)).
- *Raw power:* the Blacksmith spends cells "to raise the chance of higher-quality gear" ([wiki: Cells](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Cells)). Flask upgrades and other details: **not verified**.
- *Difficulty the player turns up:* **Boss Stem Cells**, five levels earned by beating a final boss at the current level. They bring more and harder enemies and less healing. They give back more cells (2× at 2 BSC, 3× at 4+), higher item levels, a higher forge cap, and **new areas** ([wiki: Boss Stem Cells](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Boss_Stem_Cells)).

**The pool as a problem:** players ask whether unlocking everything "dilutes your weapons pool with worse weapons" (Steam threads; these appeared in search results only and I did not open them, so **not verified**). The game's answer is **Custom Mode**, added in v1.1: you can limit the item, mutation and outfit pools and add starting gear. "Enabling less than 20 items will disable achievements" ([wiki: Custom Mode](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Custom_Mode)).

**Developer statements on progression:** not found. Interviews I opened discuss difficulty only: "We knew at the beginning we wanted something difficult, but not unfair" ([Game Informer, Dec 2018](https://www.gameinformer.com/interview/2018/12/30/dead-cells-designer-discusses-scrapped-ideas-roguelikes-and-the-potential-for)).

### 1.5 Risk of Rain 2 (Hopoo / Gearbox)

**Earned:** completing a **challenge** (an achievement) "permanently unlocks a new element" and gives 1–10 Lunar Coins. Unlocks cover 14 survivors, 30 items, 14 equipment, about 65 alternate skills, skins, and 20 artifacts (modifiers). Examples: Commando's "Godspeed" unlocks the Tactical Slide skill; Acrid's "Bad Medicine" unlocks Ravenous Bite. The page lists no challenge that grants a stat upgrade ([wiki: Challenges](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Challenges)). Because unlocks come from goals rather than currency, a lost run earns only the challenges it happened to complete.

**Spent on:** nothing; there is no meta shop for stats. What Lunar Coins buy: **not verified**. Categories: new options in the pool (items, equipment), choices before a run (survivor, alternate skills), and difficulty-or-rule modifiers (artifacts).

**Limit:** one alternate per skill slot (implied by "alternate Skill"; the selection screen is **not verified** in an opened source).

**Developer statements:** these come from the same studio, about **Risk of Rain Returns** (2023), not RoR2: "We didn't want to gate 'all the fun content' behind unlocks or force the player to earn them over time." "A lot of the unlocks ... are meant to be unlocked while playing the game naturally", while "some are meant for the player to seek out and attempt to achieve using game knowledge." "You can absolutely 'win' in Risk of Rain Returns on a fresh install." Later items "weren't strictly power upgrades, but oftentimes more complex game mechanics", and the team checked that new items "didn't replace or make other items obsolete" ([Game Developer, Apr 2024](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/risk-of-rain-interview)). No RoR2-specific rationale was found.

### 1.6 Slay the Spire (Mega Crit; early access Nov 2017, v1.0 Jan 2019)

**Earned:** "Completed or failed runs contribute points towards unlocking new characters or new relics and cards", per character ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire), no citation attached there). So a lost run earns, probably less, but the exact score formula is **not verified**.

**Spent on:** new options in the pool (cards, relics) and new characters. Nothing else.

**Difficulty the player turns up:** **Ascension**, unlocked by beating an Act 3 boss with that character. It has 20 cumulative levels, each unlocked by beating the previous one, with any unlocked level selectable. Level 1 adds more elites, level 20 adds a double boss, and the levels in between are mostly numbers and scarcity. The page names **no reward** other than the next level ([wiki: Ascension](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Ascension), Slay the Spire 1).

**Pool size:** Giovannetti reportedly judged about 75 cards per character the right balance, because more card types would make deckbuilding too haphazard ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire), citing its ref. 8; original **not verified**). For the sequel, PC Gamer's headline says an idea to *reduce* the card pool met "We need new stuff!" from players ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/slay-the-spire-2-dev-says-an-early-idea-was-to-actually-reduce-the-card-pool-but-players-hated-it-we-need-new-stuff/); body not readable).

### 1.7 Rogue Legacy 2 (Cellar Door Games; left early access in 2022, per the April 2022 articles below)

**Earned:** gold, plus blueprints and runes found in the castle. "Gold you don't spend before starting a new run is lost to Charon", unless a bought upgrade (Living Safe) keeps a percentage ([Digital Trends, May 2022](https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/rogue-legacy-2-beginners-guide)). A lost run and a won run pay the same way; the death is the normal end of each heir's run.

**Spent on:**
- *Raw power:* the castle/manor upgrades give "passive stat buffs". The Blacksmith turns blueprints into gear "that gives stat buffs to all future characters". Runes give "permanent buffs to future runs", and an upgrade allows more rune slots.
- *New options:* new classes (15; each "adds it to the random pool" of heirs), NPCs, areas and shops.
- *Cost rule:* "every upgrade you buy also increases the cost of all other upgrades" (all from [Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/rogue-legacy-2-beginners-guide)).
- *Difficulty:* **House Rules**, settings the player can turn down (or up) without penalty ([Game Rant, Apr 2022](https://gamerant.com/rogue-legacy-2-difficulty-house-rules-achievements/)). New Game+ details: **not verified**.

**Limit forcing a choice:** rune slots. Otherwise the limit is gold, and the rising cost of everything.

**How long it lasts / developer statements:** before 1.0, Teddy Lee said the economy became unmanageable around the 30th run, and announced "a huge, absolutely massive economy update that's coming in, which is purely aimed at reducing the grind" ([SVG, Apr 2022, citing Game Rant](https://www.svg.com/839150/rogue-legacy-2-the-changes-coming-to-the-1-0-release-explained/)). On the first game: "Rewarding death was a definite goal for us"; "We labeled this game a rogue-'Lite' because it takes elements from roguelikes but is a little more accessible" ([Shacknews, 2013](https://shacknews.com/article/80415/cellar-doors-teddy-lee-discusses-rogue-legacy-and-making-failure)). On House Rules, Lee says testers set enemy HP to 200%, and "We don't want people to feel bad, but it's such a stigma, right?" ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/rogue-legacy-2-difficulty-house-rules-achievements/)).

### 1.8 Vampire Survivors (extra: stats that can be undone)

PowerUps are "purchasable permanent improvements to stats that apply to all characters", bought with gold. There are 27, including Reroll, Skip, Banish and Seal, which are pool-control tools bought the same way. Buying any rank raises the price of all the others. **A "Refund PowerUps" button returns all gold**, and a maxed PowerUp can be disabled without a refund ([VS wiki: PowerUps](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/PowerUps), current to 1.15 beta, Apr 2026). **Structure the others lack:** raw stats are reversible and switchable, so a player can turn them off to test themselves or to remove Curse. Developer rationale: not found.

### 1.9 Wizard of Legend 2 (extra: a skill-kit loadout before the run)

This is the closest analogue to this project: an action roguelike where the character is a kit of skills ("Arcana"). From the guide, written for 1.0 in June 2025 and updated November 2026 ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/wizard-of-legend-2/guides/metaprogression-systems-in-wizard-of-legend-2)):
- **Unlocking a skill into the pool** (Serjik, Chaos Gems) and **building the starting loadout** (Tomi: Basic, Dash, Signature and Standard Arcana) are separate stations. Up to two more Standard Arcana come during a run, and in-run upgrades do not carry over.
- **Medallions:** pick one before the run. Some are pure positives (Vigor: more max life), some are trade-offs (Fall: −70% max health), and the basic one gives nothing.
- **Relics** are bought, and one starting relic is chosen.
- **Difficulty as runes:** boss materials buy positive and negative runes. Tiers go from 3 positive / 0 negative (+0% Chaos Gems) to 0 positive / 5 negative (+50%).
- **A Task Board** of goals gives large amounts of Chaos Gems.
- Vendor stock is random, so "you cannot reliably target a specific Arcana" (summary of a Maxroll guide, [Arcana guide](https://maxroll.gg/wizard-of-legend-2/guides/wizard-of-legend-2-arcana-guide), **not opened**).
Developer rationale: not found.

## 2. Across the games

### 2.1 Raw power between runs: the arguments

**For.**
- *It lets more players finish, and it was a stated goal.* Supergiant lists "permanent progression systems" among the features that open "the thrills of rogue-like games to more players" ([FAQ](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-faq/)). God Mode is explicitly death-scaled power: "What if we just make you a little bit tougher?", starting at an "immediately palpable" 20% ([Inverse](https://inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview)).
- *Every run gives something.* "Rewarding death was a definite goal for us" (Teddy Lee, [Shacknews 2013](https://shacknews.com/article/80415/cellar-doors-teddy-lee-discusses-rogue-legacy-and-making-failure)). Kasavin: players must "feel the time you spent wasn't a waste of your time" ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/how-supergiant-weaves-narrative-rewards-into-i-hades-i-cycle-of-perpetual-death)). Note that Kasavin's answer here is mainly *narrative* reward, not numbers.
- *Keeping players is worth a weaker core.* "a slightly weakened core loop is preferable to a player disengaging from the game" (Scott LaGrasta, [Game Developer blog, 2015](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/on-roguelikes-and-metagames)).

**Against.**
- *It weakens learning from death.* Mark R. Johnson, as summarised by LaGrasta, argues that unlocks "weaken the core loop of die, analyze, regroup". Players who progress only through unlocked FTL ships "are not learning the correct lessons from death" ([same post](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/on-roguelikes-and-metagames)). Johnson's original: **not found**.
- *Good content should not be gated, and should not be just more power.* Hopoo: "We didn't want to gate 'all the fun content' behind unlocks"; later items "weren't strictly power upgrades"; and a fresh install can win ([Game Developer 2024](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/risk-of-rain-interview)).
- *Too easy destroys the game.* Kasavin: "If you could just blow through it, what's interesting about the game goes away" ([Inverse](https://inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview)). That is why God Mode only grows "a little" with each death ([FAQ](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-faq/)). Its cap is **not verified** in an opened source.
- *Hard to pace and balance.* RL2's economy "became unmanageable around the 30th run" and needed a "massive economy update ... purely aimed at reducing the grind" ([SVG](https://www.svg.com/839150/rogue-legacy-2-the-changes-coming-to-the-1-0-release-explained/)). Shape of Dreams had to ease "excessive growth stress" on launch day, then cut mastery requirements by 30% ([Steam news](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=2444750&count=25&maxlength=4000&feeds=steam_community_announcements&enddate=1762000000)).
- *Hides whether the player improved.* I found this argued only by players (a [ResetEra thread](https://www.resetera.com/threads/im-starting-to-feel-that-stat-based-meta-progression-is-starting-to-ruin-roguelites-generally-speaking.1509337/), title seen in search, not opened) and by tool-vendor blogs ([Heroic Labs](https://heroiclabs.com/blog/what-is-meta-progression-in-games/index.html), seen in search only). **No developer source found.**
- *Makes early runs worse on purpose.* **No developer source found** stating this directly.

**Where developers disagree, without smoothing it over:**
1. Supergiant puts raw power in the meta (Mirror, God Mode) but makes it optional or a trade-off. Hopoo leaves it out entirely and argues for a winnable fresh install.
2. Lizard Smoothie moved *away* from permanent stat upgrades before launch, to equippable stars, and then was pushed by players to make progress *faster*. Making it choice-based did not remove the demand for steady growth.
3. Cellar Door built a game around stat growth, and then had to cut its grind.

**A middle position appears in practice:** stats that are visible, capped, and switchable or refundable. Examples are Hades' God Mode (opt-in, capped), Vampire Survivors' refund and disable buttons, and Grasp-limited Arcana.

### 2.2 "Unlocks dilute the pool"

**When it hurts:** when the player drafts from a random offer (3 of N) and N grows with weaker or off-build options. Dead Cells players complain about exactly this (search results, **not verified**). In this project's run, three of eight skills are offered after each floor, so every unlock directly lowers the chance of seeing a wanted skill.

**Answers found:**
- *Opt-in filtering:* Dead Cells Custom Mode limits the pools; fewer than 20 items disables achievements ([wiki](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Custom_Mode)).
- *Bought control tools:* Vampire Survivors sells Reroll, Skip, Banish and Seal as PowerUps ([wiki](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/PowerUps)). Hades' Mirror sells Fated Authority/Persuasion rerolls ([Fextralife](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Mirror+of+the+Night)).
- *Steering by loadout:* Hades keepsakes force a god's boon ([Fextralife](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Keepsakes)). Hades II's Queen card raises duo chance ([RogueRanker](https://rogueranker.com/hades-2-arcana/)).
- *Designing against it:* Hopoo checked that new items "didn't replace or make other items obsolete" ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/risk-of-rain-interview)). Slay the Spire caps the pool per character at about 75 cards ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire)).
- *Moving the unlock out of the pool into the loadout:* Wizard of Legend 2 separates "unlock into pool" from "choose starting loadout". Shape of Dreams stars and Hades aspects never enter a random pool at all; they are equipped.
- *Choosing which unlocks are active:* this is Grasp and slots again. Ownership is not the same as use.

## 3. Against the project's pillars

The pillar says "Meta mostly opens new choices, plus some raw stats in noticeable, capped steps", and forbids "Permanent upgrades of a few percent that cannot be felt."

**Fits clearly:**
- **A budget on equipped meta pieces** (Hades II Grasp; Shape of Dreams slots). Owning more opens choices; the budget keeps each choice real, and stat pieces compete with rule-changing pieces for the same space.
- **Pairs or alternatives instead of stacking** (the Hades Mirror's either/or pairs; Hades aspects; RoR2 alternate skills).
- **A separate unlock and loadout** (Wizard of Legend 2). This matches "You choose a loadout in the hub."
- **Stats as a few big, visible steps:** God Mode's opening 20% is the only sourced example of a developer explicitly aiming for "immediately palpable". Shape of Dreams' stat stars inside a slot budget also fit.
- **Unlocks earned by doing things** (RoR2 challenges; Wizard of Legend 2's Task Board). These give both lost and won runs a target beyond currency.
- **Difficulty after the first boss kill that pays in new content** (Dead Cells areas). This fits "boss ... gives something lasting that opens new choices".

**Clearly does not fit:**
- **Long chains of tiny ranks:** Hades Olympian Favor (+1% × 40), Vampire Survivors-style stat shops, RL2 manor stat lines with cost inflation. This is the pillar's anti-goal in its plainest form.
- **Uncapped stat growth as the main reward** (RL2). It also brings the grind problem its developers had to patch out.
- **Currency lost on death** (Dead Cells cells, RL2 gold). This conflicts with "Both give something lasting."
- **Large unlockable pools drafted at random without control** (the Dead Cells pre-Custom problem). This project's three-of-eight draft is very sensitive to it.

## 4. Three shapes the producer could propose

**A. Small: "the second run is different"** (pre-demo minimum)
- One currency, earned on every run; a boss kill pays a fixed bonus.
- No walkable hub: one screen between runs with two panels, *Unlock* and *Loadout*.
- *Unlock:* spend currency to add a skill to the pool (for example from 8 to 12). The boss kill also unlocks one fixed skill or elite upgrade, so the boss gives an option, not just currency.
- *Loadout:* choose which 4 unlocked skills the run starts with.
- **Cost:** 1 currency, 1 save structure, 2 panels on 1 screen, about 4 new skills.
- **What the sources warn:** each unlock dilutes the 3-of-8 draft (Dead Cells). Without a limit or stats it is short-lived and gives weaker players no help (the Hopoo approach needs a game that is winnable fresh).

**B. Middle: "Grasp for an instrument"**
- Everything in A, plus a **budgeted meta rack**: owned pieces cost points to equip, and the budget rises with progress (the Grasp model).
- Pieces are of two kinds: capped stat steps (for example +15% health, three ranks at most, each clearly felt) and rule changers (a starting mark, focus starting at 25%, an extra reroll of a skill offer).
- Pool control is sold as pieces too (reroll, or ban one skill from offers), so dilution is answered by the same system.
- Run-end goals ("kill the boss with 3 shotgun skills") unlock pieces, as in RoR2 challenges and the Wizard of Legend 2 task board.
- **Cost:** 1–2 currencies, about 3 panels (Unlock, Loadout, Rack), a goal tracker, 10–15 rack pieces.
- **What the sources warn:** pacing has to be tuned with players. Shape of Dreams patched growth on launch day, and RL2 found its economy breaking around run 30. Two currencies multiply that risk.

**C. Full: "Crossroads"** (post-demo)
- Everything in B, plus a small walkable hub with stations: unlock, loadout, rack, crafting of new run features (Hades II incantations), skill variants (Hades aspects and RoR2 alternate skills applied to single skills).
- Player-raised difficulty after the first boss kill: Pact-style conditions or Wizard of Legend 2-style negative runes, paying in currency and later in **new content** (Dead Cells areas, extra variants).
- A per-skill or per-class mastery track that unlocks slots (Shape of Dreams).
- **Cost:** 3+ currencies, 5–7 stations or screens, a difficulty system, a variant system for each skill, an NPC/hub scene. This conflicts with the "no town" and "no story" pillar exclusions before the demo.
- **What the sources warn:** content-hungry. Hades II's incantations depend on many gathered reagents, some "quite pricey" ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/hades-2-incantations-unlock-first-tips-1851461926)). Shape of Dreams needed launch-day fixes for the economy. Heat gives little back unless something new is attached (Slay the Spire gives only the next level).

## 5. Questions the design must answer, in order

1. **Does an unlocked skill enter the random offer pool, or only become choosable in the loadout?** This decides whether dilution exists at all.
2. **Is there a budget on equipped meta pieces (Grasp/slots), and does it rise?** This is the main tool for keeping "mostly choices".
3. **What exactly do a death and a boss kill each give?** The pillar demands that both give lasting options. Is the boss reward a guaranteed unlock?
4. **How big is one stat step, and where is the hard cap?** The only sourced benchmark for "palpable" is 20% (God Mode).
5. **How many runs should the demo's meta last before it is used up,** and how will that be tuned? Both RL2 and Shape of Dreams retuned after release.
6. **How many currencies?** One is cheapest; each extra one is another economy to tune.
7. **Is the hub a screen or a place for the demo?** A screen fits the "no town" exclusion.
8. **When does player-raised difficulty open, and what does it pay?** Currency, or new content?
9. **Does demo progress carry into the release?** Shape of Dreams: yes. Hades II test: no.
10. **Do elite-room skill upgrades become lasting unlocks too,** or stay purely in-run?
