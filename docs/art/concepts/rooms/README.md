# Room mood concepts

Five pictures drawn by Codex on 2026-11-20 from the project's style reference, after Kristian asked that the rooms "feel more alive and create atmosphere like Diablo and Path of Exile" in the drawing style the game has. He looked at them the same evening and chose: **build toward them.**

| File | Room |
|---|---|
| `room_treasure_vault.png` | A treasure room with the corridor outside: gold light out of the doorway, a chest sign over the arch |
| `room_tomb_hall.png` | Tomb hall: two rows of sarcophagi, a knight's statue as the eye-catcher |
| `room_shrine.png` | Shrine: an altar under one cold shaft of light |
| `room_ossuary.png` | Ossuary: walls of bones, a hanging cage, a misty pit |
| `room_throne.png` | The throne room that ends a floor: the bone throne on a dais, a long carpet, teal braziers |

What they do that the game did not yet: darkness between the light sources (warm candle pools against cold teal), low fog, dust and embers in the air, a worn floor (cracks, missing tiles, rubble, bones, wax), and one eye-catcher per room, with the middle of the floor kept clear for the fight.

They are targets for mood, not plans: the rooms are generated, and their sizes and doors are the generator's.
