# Floor plan concepts

Three pictures drawn by Codex on the night of 2026-11-20, after Kristian said the rooms are "very clear squares" and that a floor should feel like a dungeon crawl, "not large office landscapes with monsters in them". Each shows one whole floor from straight above: one route from the entrance (teal dot) to the throne room (red dot).

| File | Idea |
|---|---|
| `floor_plan_1_chambers_and_passages.png` | Rooms keep a rectangular core but have cut corners, alcoves and apses, pillar rows, low broken walls inside, and short narrow passages between them |
| `floor_plan_2_cathedral_crypt.png` | One bent axis with round and octagonal chapels, a cross-shaped hall, side chapels as pockets |
| `floor_plan_3_ruined_catacomb.png` | The same route through collapsed and half-natural spaces, with only the throne room whole |

He has not chosen between them. The research report `docs/research/reports/d-how-dungeon-floors-are-built.md` proposes how the generator could get there; its proposal A is the first picture's idea.
