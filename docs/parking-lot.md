# Parking lot

Ideas and direction changes wait here until the next milestone.

- **Accuracy that rewards standing still** (Kristian, 2026-11-19). Shots are perfectly accurate today whether she stands or runs. His idea: add accuracy later, so that it pays to stand still to hit. He raised it when he said yes to steering the skill slide with the move keys: that is a little like counter-strafing, which only means something once there is accuracy to win. An opposite key braking the slide belongs here too. See `docs/decisions/0004-skills-on-the-move.md`.
- **Taking cover** (Kristian, 2026-11-19, the evening walls and tall props began to stop shots). His words: "en slags cover-mekanikk. Med control-knappen, lene seg inntil vegger/props med litt høyde for å dukke unna skudd som kommer på avstand ... det er i hvert fall en mechanic som er morro, så vi kan utforske det." To be tried as an experiment behind a switch, after the enemy models are in and the floors have many more shooters. The producer's first shape for it: hold Ctrl near a wall or a prop and she slides to it and ducks; ducked, low props (under 1.2 m) count as cover too; shooting or using a skill stands her up for as long as it lasts; slow movement along the cover while Ctrl is held. The risk to watch: it must not slow the game down (pillar 2); melee enemies running round the cover are the counterweight.
